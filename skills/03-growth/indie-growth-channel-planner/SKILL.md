# Indie Growth Channel Planner

Use this skill to choose channels instead of spraying every platform.

## Channel Map

- **SEO**: tool sites, converters, template libraries, comparison pages, long-term traffic.
- **Reddit**: niche consumer pain, AI tools, developer workflows, but high ban risk.
- **YouTube SEO**: tutorial/problem keywords and product demos.
- **TikTok / short video**: visual apps, habit tools, AI outputs, before/after demos.
- **X**: developer tools, AI builders, indie maker storytelling.
- **GitHub**: open-source-led products, dev tools, CLI, desktop utilities.
- **V2EX / Chinese communities**: early technical users, especially for dev/AI products.
- **Xiaohongshu**: Chinese users with overseas purchasing power, creator/workflow products.
- **BetaList / Product Hunt / There's An AI For That / Toolify / AlternativeTo**: launch visibility and directory discovery.
- **AppSumo / lifetime deal**: cold-start paid validation for SaaS with low marginal cost.
- **Trustpilot / review platforms**: credibility and review loops after first users.
- **Blogs/backlinks**: SEO authority and comparisons.
- **Influencer ads**: useful when channel-user fit is clear and budget exists.

## Strategy Rules

- Different products need different channels.
- A nofollow link can still be valuable if it brings real users.
- For SEO, non-brand keyword ranking matters more than only brand search.
- For AI tools, directory and creator discovery can matter early.
- For visual products, make a demo before writing long copy.
- For developer tools, open-source proof and GitHub trust can be a distribution asset.
- For paid ads or influencer campaigns, define conversion and cost caps first.
